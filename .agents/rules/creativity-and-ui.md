# SQUI Creativity, Branding & UI/UX Guidelines

## 1. Mascot Identity & Tone
* The SQUI mascot is a mindful, intelligent squirrel that emphasizes preparation, balance, and small daily wins.
* Tone must remain encouraging, non-judgmental, warm, and educational.
* SQUI Motto: "Awareness over restriction. Progress over perfection. Education over judgment."

## 2. Color Palette & Typography
* **Primary**: Emerald Green (`#10B981`, `#059669`) — representing vitality, fresh produce, and health.
* **Secondary / Mascot Accent**: Warm Amber Orange (`#F59E0B`, `#EA580C`) — energy, optimism, and squirrel warmth.
* **Backgrounds**: Warm Oatmeal/Cream (`#FDFBF7`) for light mode, Charcoal Slate (`#0F172A`) for dark mode.
* **Cards & Surfaces**: Glassmorphic translucent surfaces with crisp borders (`rgba(0,0,0,0.06)` or `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`).

## 3. UI Dynamics & Micro-interactions
* Instant visual feedback for meal additions, weight logging, and water intake.
* Progress gauges with smooth animations and intuitive color transitions (Green -> Amber -> Red-Orange warning).
* Visual Food Diary displayed as an inviting photo stream with time and category badges.
